Guacamole

Description

Guacamole is a Mexican dip or spread that originated in the south of Mexico. It's made from mashed avocados and is often served with tortilla chips or as a topping for tacos.

Ingredients

  • 3 avocados - peeled, pitted, and mashed
  • 1 lime - juiced
  • 1 teaspoon salt or to taste
  • 2 roma tomatoes - diced
  • 1/2 cup diced onion
  • 3 tablespoons chopped fresh cilantro
  • 1 jalapeno pepper - seeded and chopped (optional)

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Mash avocados, lime juice, and salt together in a medium bowl; mix in tomatoes, onion, cilantro, and jalapeno.
  3. Serve immediately with tortilla chips, or cover and refrigerate for 1 hour for improved flavor.
